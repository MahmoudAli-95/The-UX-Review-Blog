**Short Description**

**The UX Review** is a high-energy, neubrutalist web platform delivering raw, unfiltered content on technology, design, and digital culture. Designed with bold neo-brutalist styling—featuring thick borders, high-contrast colors, offset shadows, and playful micro-interactions—it provides readers with fresh articles, author highlights, interactive categories, and community newsletter enrollment.

### ✨ Features & Highlights

* **🔥 Hero Section:** Eye-catching rotated badges, high-contrast typography, floating accent elements, and dynamic call-to-action buttons.
* **📰 Article Feed:** Dynamic cards styled with custom border shadows, badges (`NEW`, `HOT`, `VIRAL`), reading time estimates, and article engagement metrics.
* **🎯 Trending & Categories:** Floating sidebar featuring top-trending reads and interactive category pills (`TECH`, `DESIGN`, `CULTURE`, etc.).
* **✍️ Meet the Authors:** Featured author cards highlighting creators, titles, bios, and social media links.
* **💬 Community & Reviews:** Member benefits overview, real-time feedback cards from top industry professionals, and direct join buttons.
* **📬 Newsletter Integration:** Dark-themed footer with a direct email subscription form and site links.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Design

* **🎨 Styling:** Custom CSS3 leveraging a Neo-Brutalist aesthetic (bold 2px/4px borders, hard offset box-shadows, vivid color palette).
* **📱 Layout:** Fully responsive CSS Grid & Flexbox setup adapted for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
* **🔤 Typography & Icons:** Powered by the *Exo* Google Font family and Font Awesome icons.
